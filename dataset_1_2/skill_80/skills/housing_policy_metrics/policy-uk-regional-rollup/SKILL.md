## When This Skill Applies
Use for multi-nation charts displaying separate curves or bars for all four UK nations.

## Preconditions
- Separate series for England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland must appear on the chart.

## Procedure
1. Sum annual figures across all four nations and compute each nation's percentage share.
