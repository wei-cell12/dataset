## When This Skill Applies
Use when a chart reports bachelor's-degree completion shares specifically for ages 25–29 across demographic groups.
## Procedure
1. Extract each demographic group's printed percentage.
2. Compute the highest-to-lowest percentage-point gap.
3. Emit JSON with `COHORT_SCOPE: AGES_25_TO_29`.
